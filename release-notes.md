## 0.3.0 (2026-11-22)


###   BREAKING CHANGES

* **web:** aichan.create refuses the params network and title, and aichan.list answers no network.
* **flows:** `hmz exec -f ralph_loop` (and the other five) is refused until the flow is installed from the official flowverse. `hmz.flows.builtin` is gone, as are `hmz.runtime.flowing.BUILTIN_AT` and `builtin`, and `hmz.runtime.flowing.index.RESERVED` and `reserved`; `hmz.runtime.flowing.verses.holds` returns one `Path`.
* a side conversation now runs inside a fence, and is refused on a machine that cannot hold one.
* **outworlder:** the chat flow and flows.check.chats are gone.
* **settings:** drop the btw setting, and carry ai_chan between daemons ([#706](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/706))
* **runtime:** make Ai-chan's conversation a chat playing her role, with her tools ([#690](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/690))
* **chat:** the chat flow and flows.check.chats are gone.
* **tui:** `/btw` off a session view is refused, pointing at `/ai`, and the General page no longer sets the `btw` agent.
* **daemon:** `epic.aside` no longer takes `asks` or `answered`; `btw.ask` needs `to`; `btw.list` no longer answers `default`, nor `btw.ask` `asked`.
* **protocol:** style-src now admits 'unsafe-inline', and env.* calls no longer wait on a subscription's other calls.
* **protocol:** specify Ai-chan, reach and env methods ([#673](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/673))
* **coganchor:** an anchor or cog of an earlier release no longer meets one of this release over tcp:// or peer://.
* **nous:** replace fallback chains with nous and nous graphs ([#645](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/645))
* **protocol:** a stdio: client no longer reaches an `hmz daemon connect --stdio` of an earlier release, which writes no sync line.
* `hmz server` is removed; a daemon listening on
* **desktop:** server.rs and the macOS install path are gone; the app needs a page that speaks RPC over the pipes ([#636](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/636)).
* **web:** the page is a JSON-RPC client of every daemon it shows ([#636](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/636))
* **tui:** the daemon form no longer writes `via`/`host`/EasyTier-secret entries; daemons are written as routes and a pinned id.
* the CLI and the SDK as daemon clients; remove HUMANIZE_DAEMON ([#633](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/633))
* **runtime:** a remote daemon is no longer an hmz web reached over ssh -L; the HTTP members of Remotes kept for the web and the TUI reach nothing until Wave 4 ports them.
* **daemon:** speak JSON-RPC on the socket and on listeners, through internal host links ([#624](https://github.com/humanfia/humanize/issues/624))
* **coganchor:** the anchor no longer runs as Python. Without a packaged `coganchor`, a checkout's `crates/coganchor/target` build, or `HUMANIZE_COGANCHOR`, no anchored turn runs.
* **protocol:** PermissionKind and the local/user/system/online fields of Permission are removed from the protocol.
* **web:** POST /api/btw takes a required aside and answers it; GET /api/btw answers asides in place of open and said, filtered by epic; POST /api/btw/leave takes aside in place of to. A conversation of an earlier epic is answered as to <agent>/<n>, no longer <epic>/<agent>/<n>.
* **btw:** The Epic is answered by the `btw` setting alone. With it unset, a side question of The Epic is refused rather than answered by the flow's first agent: set it in Settings : General (web) or /settings : General : /btw The Epic (terminal). An epic's own btw agent, `POST /api/btw/agent` and btw mode's ctrl+g are gone, and `GET /api/btw` answers no `agent`.
* **runtime:** runtimes take `harness` instead of `affinity`, and work on another machine with no setting no longer drops to a harness here when the CLI is missing or a gating hook is hung: set the runtime's harness to `local`. A saved `affinity` is still read, as its first entry with `self` as `env`.
